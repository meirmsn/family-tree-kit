# People

Append-only. One `## P<n> Name` section per person (P1 = home person). Every fact: source ID +
label (confirmed / probable / possible / lore). Corrections go in a new pass section or in
"Reading corrections"; never edit old text. Conflicts are written down as conflicts.

The heading may keep the spelling the family used. On the site, the title follows the earliest or
most official record; a different family spelling goes in parentheses after it; every other
documented spelling is listed with its source. A suspected same person stays a possible match until
the user says to merge. See METHODOLOGY.md §2.9.
