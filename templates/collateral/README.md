# Collateral lines

Relatives who are **not direct ancestors of P1** (siblings, aunts, uncles, cousins and their
families, witnesses, neighbours) and any *candidate* direct ancestors whose identification depends
on them. They often left better records than the direct ancestors.

## The rule
1. Collateral people get **C-numbers** (C1, C2, …), unique across this folder. They are **not** added
   to people.md's main P-list.
2. Direct-line facts in people.md are **not upgraded** from collateral evidence until the promotion
   rule is met.
3. **Promotion rule:** the collateral person's link to our ancestor is at least **probable**, and the
   record naming the earlier generation is at least **probable** (ideally one record stating the
   relationship directly, or two independent records that agree). Then the promotion is **proposed**
   in the pass report and in people.md. It is **not applied until the user agrees**; the applying
   pass records the approval time and keeps a pointer (`C2 → P31`).
4. Collateral records still get full images, crops, sidecars and index entries, tagged
   `collateral: true`.
5. Every fact keeps a source and a label. Files are append-only.
6. Living people: record only what the historical records show.
