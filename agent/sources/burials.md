# Burials and gravestones

| Source | What | Access | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| JOWBR (JewishGen Online Worldwide Burial Registry) — https://www.jewishgen.org/databases/cemetery/ | burial indexes and photos worldwide, incl. some Latin American communities | free login | Browse by cemetery as well as by name: a "not found" by name can be a spelling problem. |
| Find a Grave — https://www.findagrave.com/ | user-contributed memorials and photos | free | User data: check against records. |
| BillionGraves — https://billiongraves.com/ | GPS-tagged stone photos, transcribed | free | |
| Community burial societies and yahrzeit lists | local search pages, published lists | varies | Many communities publish searchable lists; some block automated browsers: log and ask the user. |

Hebrew gravestones give the **father's name** (X son/daughter of Y) and the Hebrew date: convert it
and record both. A matching surname in the right city is only *possible* until the family or a
record links it; the family can also unlink it.
