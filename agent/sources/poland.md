# Poland (Russian partition / Congress Poland; also Prussian and Austrian parts now in Poland)

| Source | What | Access | How to query / notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| JRI-Poland — https://jri-poland.org/ | index of Jewish vital records 1808–1940s from Polish archives | free; donations unlock some data | **Leads only** until the act image is read. Legacy search (`legacy.jri-poland.org/databases/jridetail_2.php?gub=<code>&srch1=<surname>&srch1v=S&srch1t=<D|Q|S>`) works without login; `tools/polite_query.py jri <gub> <surname>`. **Validate the gubernia/region code** against the site's list: an invalid code returns zero hits that look like a negative. Rows give archive, akta/year, act number → go to the scan. |
| Geneteka — https://geneteka.genealodzy.pl/ | volunteer index of Polish parish and civil registers (all religions in many areas) | free | **Leads only.** Choose the province (region code), then surname. Results sometimes link to scans — open the scan before citing. Tag Geneteka/search URLs `lead; index only` until the act image is read. |
| Szukajwarchiwach — https://www.szukajwarchiwach.gov.pl/ | Polish State Archives catalogue **and scans** (incl. many Jewish civil registers, AGAD) | free | Search fonds ("Akta stanu cywilnego …"), open the unit (*jednostka*), browse scans. Read the yearly index first, then the act. The catalogue sometimes mislabels units: check content, not the label. Heavy crawling triggers "portlet temporarily unavailable" or redirects: one page at a time, 2–3 s apart, in a real browser. |
| AGAD (Central Archives of Historical Records, Warsaw) — https://agad.gov.pl/ | incl. Galician Jewish registers (Fond 300) | free scans via szukajwarchiwach | See galicia-ukraine.md. |
| Polish State Archive branches | records not online, records < 100 years | email request | Use agent/briefs/archive-request.md. Civil records under 100 years old (births) / 80 (marriages, deaths) are usually still at the town civil registry office (USC). |
| Lubgens / PTG / regional society mirrors | indexes and scan mirrors for some regions | free | Check what each covers. |

Reading: acts 1868–1917 in Russian with double (Julian/Gregorian) dates; 1808–1867 in Polish.
Late registrations and twins registered together are common.

**Index hits are leads only.** Geneteka, JRI and similar search/index URLs must not be cited as proof of a fact until the original register scan is read. See METHODOLOGY §3.2 and `docs/research-process-lessons.md` §4.
