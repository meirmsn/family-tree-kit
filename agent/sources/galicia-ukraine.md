# Galicia (Austrian Poland, today southern Poland and western Ukraine) and Ukraine

| Source | What | Access | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gesher Galicia — All Galicia Database (https://search.geshergalicia.org/) | indexes of vital records, cadastral and tax lists, voter lists, inventories of AGAD Fond 300 | free search; membership for some | Its inventory says which towns' books survive and where (AGAD Warsaw vs Ukrainian oblast archives). |
| AGAD Fond 300 via szukajwarchiwach | scans of Galician Jewish metrical books (many 1870s–1900s) | free | Tables in German/Polish; earlier books often in German Kurrent script. Births of a whole district were sometimes kept in the district-seat book. |
| JRI-Poland (Galicia indexes) | indexes of AGAD and some Ukrainian holdings | free | As in poland.md. |
| Ukrainian State Archives (oblast archives) | metrical books, census, notarial records | email request; some scans online | Some fonds are scanned and published (on archive sites or Wikimedia Commons). Expect slow replies; ask cost first. |
| FamilySearch films of Ukrainian/Galician records | Greek/Roman Catholic and some Jewish books | login | Check the catalogue by town. |

Pitfall: town names exist in several districts (and were renamed after 1945); confirm the district.
