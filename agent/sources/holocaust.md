# Holocaust-era records

| Source | What | Access | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yad Vashem Central Database of Shoah Victims' Names — https://collections.yadvashem.org/en/names | Pages of Testimony (often name parents, spouse, maiden names, the submitter), lists | free | Pages of Testimony are among the best sources for a generation lost in the war; the submitter is often a relative (a lead — you contact no one). Cite the record id. Mass queries are throttled: go slowly. |
| Arolsen Archives — https://collections.arolsen-archives.org/ | camp, DP and post-war tracing records | free | Good for survivors and displaced persons (incl. IRO). |
| USHMM Holocaust Survivors and Victims Database | lists and records | free | |
| JDC Archives names index — https://archives.jdc.org/ | aid recipients, emigration lists | free | |
| Yizkor (memorial) books — https://www.jewishgen.org/yizkor/ ; NYPL scans on archive.org (OCR) | town histories, necrologies, photos | free | Necrology lists give names and sometimes relations; cite the page. |
| JewishGen Holocaust database | many lists | free login | |
