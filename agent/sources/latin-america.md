# Latin America

Starter list; Jewish and immigrant communities in Latin America left civil records, naturalisation
files, community burial lists and passenger arrivals. Spanish double surnames (father's + mother's)
give each parent's surname: use them in searches.

| Country / source | What | Access | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| FamilySearch (all countries) | civil registration and parish images for many countries | login | The main free route for civil records in most countries. |
| Argentina — CEMLA (Centro de Estudios Migratorios Latinoamericanos) | immigrant arrivals at Buenos Aires | free search | |
| Brazil — Arquivo Nacional (SIAN) | immigration and naturalisation | free | |
| Mexico — Archivo General de la Nación; FamilySearch civil registration | immigration cards, civil records | free / login | |
| Costa Rica — Archivo Nacional (ANCR) catalogue; Tribunal Supremo de Elecciones civil registry consultations | court files, naturalisation, notarial records; civil registry lookups | free | The civil registry lookup may block automated browsers: log it and ask the user. |
| Venezuela, Colombia, Peru, Chile, Uruguay, Cuba | civil registration (largely via FamilySearch), community lists | varies | Community newspapers and yahrzeit lists help date deaths. |
| JOWBR (see burials.md) | some Latin American Jewish cemeteries | free login | |
| Community archives and newspapers | announcements, yahrzeit lists, membership lists | varies | Context and dating; cite the issue/page. |

Records under 100 years old may be restricted: ask the office for its procedure (archive-request brief).
