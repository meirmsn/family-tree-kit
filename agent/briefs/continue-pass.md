## Recursion rule
Every new person a record names (parent, witness, informant, grandparent) becomes a **new gap** for
the next round. Each finding spawns the next lead until every filmed book for the relevant towns and
years has been read. Do not stop at the first hit for the target person.

## End of round / saturation
- Report new finds, one-line negatives, and an updated **"Still open online"** list
  (`open-questions.md`).
- A line is **saturated** only when a full sweep of every reachable online source produced nothing
  new **and** that list is empty for the line.
- A catalogue unit that exists but has **0 scans** is an **archive request**, not a negative (log it
  under gaps that need an archive, not under negatives).

## Online exhaustion
When "Still open online" is empty for the lines in scope, write an *online exhaustion summary*
(`templates/online-exhaustion.md` → project `online-exhaustion.md`) and turn the remaining gaps into
archive-request drafts (`archive-request.md`). Never send them without the user's OK.
